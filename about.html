About CognaBright

Practical support for everyday life.

CognaBright helps families and support teams bring routines, communication, goals, progress and care-team collaboration together — personalised around the person, and connected with the people who support them.

How we think about this work

Support, not a game or a lifestyle product.

CognaBright is built around real, everyday support — not around competition, celebration for its own sake, or the idea that support needs are a lifestyle to sell. We talk about independence, communication, routines, progress and collaboration, and we let families and professionals make their own choices about what's right for them.

We don't promise outcomes, and we don't romanticise or trivialise disability. We aim to make everyday support clearer, more consistent and more connected — for the people receiving support, and the people around them.

Working principles

How we handle what we say publicly.

1

Traceability

Material public statements map to working product or reviewed evidence through a claims registry.

2

Proportion

3

Permission
