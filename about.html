About Cogna Bright

Development with an evidence boundary.

Cogna Bright is developing a digital platform and a partnership pathway focused on responsible learning before broad commercial or outcome claims.

Why this website changed

The audience is partnership-first.

The public website is designed primarily for universities, researchers and organisations that may contribute independent, practice-informed scrutiny. Families and providers can read about possible future pilots, without being offered an unsupported trial or paid plan.

Public wording is intentionally narrower than a product roadmap. The website does not turn proposed features, mock-ups or aspirations into present-tense capabilities.

Working principles

How public information is handled.

1

Traceability

Material public statements map to code or reviewed evidence through a claims registry.

2

Proportion

Developmental work is described as developmental; future evaluation is not described as completed research.

3

Permission

Names, logos, endorsements and participant stories should not appear without specific approval and evidence review.
