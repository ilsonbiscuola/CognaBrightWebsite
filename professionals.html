For professionals

Useful support for therapists, educators and teams.

Cogna Bright is being designed to complement professional support by making routines clearer, evidence easier to organise and progress easier to discuss.

  • Support consistent communication practice.
  • Log useful observations tied to routines and goals.
  • Review progress snapshots with families and support teams.
  • Use one practical system across everyday moments.

Cogna Bright is not yet commercially released and this website is a preview.
