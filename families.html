For families

Support at home, in routines that matter.

Cogna Bright is being shaped to help families support everyday routines with more confidence, less guesswork and more visible progress over time.

  • Support daily living routines such as showering, dressing, bedtime and brushing teeth.
  • Use practical communication supports linked to real moments.
  • Keep simple records of what worked and what still needs support.
  • Share useful information with the professionals around your child.
