Selected features

Product areas we are shaping

These are early product directions, not final promises. The goal is to show the kinds of support Cogna Bright is being built around.

Communication

Visual supports, spoken prompts and routine-linked communication practice.

Daily routines

Shower, dressing, brushing teeth, bedtime and other practical life routines.

Music Therapy

Meaningful engagement ideas using music and structured interaction.

Progress tracking

Goals, logs, snapshots and progress visuals designed to be easier to understand.

Responsive access

Designed for phones, tablets and laptops.

Role-based usefulness

Useful for families, therapists, support workers, educators and teams around the child.
