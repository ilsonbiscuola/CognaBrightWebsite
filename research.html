Research pilots

Interested in a future research pilot?

Separate from using CognaBright as a family or organisation today, we occasionally run formal research pilots with universities and services. Expressing interest is a contact record, not enrolment — no pilot place, eligibility or start date is promised, and future eligibility criteria may differ from what applies today.

Please don't include a child's name, diagnosis, records, or any client, patient or student information in an enquiry. Read the website privacy notice for how enquiries are handled.
