Evidence and claims

Know the status behind the statement.

Cogna Bright uses a public status framework and a repository marketing-claims registry to separate verified website capabilities from developmental intentions and future evaluation.

Status framework

Four labels with different meanings.

Verified now

Traceable to working code in the audited marketing repository or explicit approved evidence. Verification is scoped to the statement shown.

In development

A product direction or capability under active development. Availability, completeness and suitability are not implied.

Planned for evaluation

A question, study or pilot pathway being prepared. Approval, commencement, completion and findings are not implied.

Not claimed

A statement deliberately withheld because evidence is absent, insufficient, unapproved or outside the audited scope.

Current public position

What can be said today.

Verified now

Partnership enquiry workflow

This website implements validated, server-side enquiry handling with a honeypot, request-size limits, consent capture and server-only database credentials.

Planned for evaluation

Independent evaluation

Cogna Bright is inviting conversations about future independent evaluation; no completed study or finding is represented.

In development

Platform capabilities

Platform direction is described only in future-facing or developmental language until implementation and approval evidence is reviewed.

Not claimed

Results and institutional proof

No statistics, testimonials, customer counts, partner logos, clinical outcomes, case-study results, pricing, integrations, certifications, service levels, return-on-investment or time-saving claims are published.

Publication control

Claims require traceability.

Material public statements carry a claim identifier in page markup. Automated tests check that each identifier exists in the repository registry and is approved for publication at its stated status.

Future evidence should be reviewed for scope, source quality, permission, recency and wording before a claim changes status. A product roadmap, mock-up or marketing plan is not treated as evidence that a capability exists.
