What an expression of interest means

A contact record, not enrolment.

Submitting the partnership form allows Cogna Bright to review your high-level interest and, if you separately opt in, send relevant updates. It does not enrol anyone in research or a pilot.

  • Do not submit a child’s name, diagnosis, records or other sensitive information.
  • Do not submit client, patient or student information.
  • Future eligibility criteria may differ from your circumstances.
  • You may request correction or deletion of your website enquiry data.
