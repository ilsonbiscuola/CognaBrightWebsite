How CognaBright supports families

  • Practical support for everyday life

    Routines, visual supports, communication tools and everyday progress in one place.

  • Personalised for each child

    Create supports that reflect each child's needs, strengths and everyday routines.

  • Communication and independence

    Visual tools can help make everyday activities clearer and easier to understand and follow.

  • Care-team connection

    Keep helpful information and updates together for the people supporting your child.

    Keep important goals, everyday achievements and progress visible.
