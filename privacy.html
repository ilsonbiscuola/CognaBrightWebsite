Website privacy notice

Privacy for partnership enquiries.

This notice describes the public marketing website only. It does not describe data handling inside the separate Cogna Bright application.

Last reviewed: 23 July 2026. This operational notice still requires legal review before production publication.

Information we collect

When you submit the partnership form, we collect your name, email, institution or organisation, organisation type, role, country or region, enquiry type, area of interest, message, privacy consent and optional updates preference. The service also processes limited technical information needed for abuse prevention, such as request timing and network address.

Please do not submit health information or information that identifies a child, client, patient, participant or student.

Why we use it

We use the information to review and respond to your enquiry, maintain a record of partnership conversations, prevent misuse of the form and—only when you choose the optional checkbox—send relevant updates.

How it is handled

The browser sends the form to a Cogna Bright server endpoint. Database credentials remain server-side. Enquiry records are stored in the connected database and are not available for anonymous public reading through the website.

Access should be limited to people who need the information to handle enquiries or maintain the service. We do not publish form submissions.

Cookies and analytics

This repository does not implement advertising trackers, behavioural analytics, heat maps or a newsletter service. The form does not set a marketing cookie. Hosting and security providers may still process ordinary request logs as part of delivering the site.

Retention

Enquiry retention periods have not yet been legally approved. Until a schedule is approved, records should be reviewed periodically and removed when no longer needed for the enquiry or a documented legal purpose. This page does not promise a fixed deletion timeframe.

Your choices

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of your website enquiry information, subject to identity verification and any applicable legal requirements. You can decline optional updates and still submit a partnership enquiry.

Contact

Use the partnership enquiry form and choose “General partnership question”. Do not include additional sensitive information in your request.

This is a website-specific operational privacy notice, pending legal review.
