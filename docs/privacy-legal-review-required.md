# Privacy Policy — legal decisions still required before launch

`privacy.html` is a complete draft, written to be truthful and non-specific wherever an exact
answer is not yet approved. It must not be treated as final production legal copy until the
items below are reviewed and resolved. None of these are displayed to public users — see the
HTML comment at the top of `privacy.html` for the pointer back to this file.

- Approved data-retention periods (the page currently uses non-specific "as long as reasonably
  necessary" language rather than an invented number of days/months/years).
- Applicable Australian privacy-law wording (e.g. whether the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the
  Australian Privacy Principles apply, and any small-business-exemption analysis).
- Children's/family-support-specific privacy wording (the page states an adult manages the
  account on the supported person's behalf, but hasn't been legally reviewed for sufficiency).
- International processing/storage wording (the page uses a general statement; confirm whether
  more specific disclosures or safeguards, e.g. for EU/UK users, are required).
- Deletion-request handling process and timeframe.
- Subprocessor list (hosting, authentication, subscription-billing and any other processors) —
  not currently named individually.
- Cookies/analytics actually used inside the CognaBright application (confirmed: none on the
  marketing website; NOT confirmed for the application itself — the current wording is
  deliberately generic and should be replaced with an accurate, specific disclosure).
- Data-breach notification process and commitments.
- Family vs. organisation vs. CognaBright controller/processor responsibility wording, including
  for information organisations manage about the people they support.

Once these are resolved, update `privacy.html` directly and remove the HTML comment marking it
as a draft pending review.
