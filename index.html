Why families and organisations choose CognaBright

Practical, person-centred and connected.

Private and secure

Your information stays in your control. CognaBright uses secure systems and sharing follows your choices and permissions.

Personalised, not generic

Routines, communication and goals are built around each person, not a one-size-fits-all template.

Keeps everyone connected

Families, support workers and professionals can stay on the same page through care-team collaboration.

Grounded in evidence, not hype

We say clearly what CognaBright does and does not claim.

CognaBright does not claim to be clinically proven, independently validated, or used by a specific number of families. We publish a clear evidence-status framework so you can see what is verified, what is still developing, and what has not been claimed.
