Built for real life

Real object first. Real communication. Real independence.

Cogna Bright is being designed for the moments that matter most — not just screen time. The goal is to support children in everyday routines, help adults work consistently together, and make progress easier to understand.

🗓️
Supports everyday routines

Practical tools for showering, bedtime, getting dressed, communication practice and other daily living moments.

👥

Families, therapists, support workers and educators can follow the same routines and language.

📊
Makes progress visible

Future case scenarios

How Cogna Bright could make a difference

1. Family at home

Daily routines with more confidence

A parent can support showering, dressing or bedtime with a clear step-by-step routine, visual support and one place to review what worked.

2. Therapist session

Communication practice with useful evidence

A therapist can practise requests, monitor responses and log evidence in a way that makes sessions easier to review later.

3. Shared progress

Everyone stays aligned

Progress snapshots, routines and goals can help families and professionals celebrate wins together and stay more consistent.

Product highlights

Selected features planned for Cogna Bright

Visual communication support

Support expression, choices and functional communication through practical visual tools linked to real routines.

Daily living routines

Structure for shower, brushing teeth, getting dressed, bedtime and other everyday independence skills.

Music Therapy

Music-based engagement ideas designed to help participation, enjoyment and shared interaction.

Goals and progress

Track goals, routine performance and evidence over time in a more organised and meaningful way.

Responsive experience

Designed to work across mobile, tablet and laptop so support can happen where life happens.

Bilingual potential

Support for English and Portuguese as part of a more inclusive and practical family experience.
